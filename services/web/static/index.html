Hybrid Vision:
Next-Generation Drone Detection.

Combining RGB and event vision for robust, real-time drone detection in challenging environments.

The Challenge.

Reliable perception is difficult under real-world conditions. Conventional RGB cameras suffer from motion blur, low-light environments, and rapid illumination changes, often resulting in missed detections and reduced system reliability.

Smart Fusion.

Our hybrid fusion architecture combines conventional RGB imagery with high-frequency event data to deliver reliable drone detection where traditional vision systems reach their limits.

sequence_127 val 47.5 s99.5 M2,160 12.6 s Validation only — never used for training. Shortest usable duration (47.5 s vs ~108–117 s for train sequences). Very high burst ratio (59%) — most events in this sequence are camera noise. Drone appears 7.6 s after the noise skip.
Camera noise burst — when a Prophesee event camera powers on, unsettled sensor pixels fire continuously for several seconds. All sequences skip the first 5 s (start_s = 5.0) to discard this burst; sequence_124 uses start_s = 7.0 due to a residual burst at 5–7 s. Events before start_s are excluded from reconstruction and training.
E2VID LSTM warmup — after the burst skip, the reconstruction network starts from a zero hidden state and takes ~20 s to converge. During this period reconstructed frames appear visually degraded, but YOLO detections remain accurate (verified on sequence_85: centre error <5 px throughout).
