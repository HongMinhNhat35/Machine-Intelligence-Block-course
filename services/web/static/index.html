Hybrid Vision:
Next-Generation Drone Detection.

Combining RGB and event vision for robust, real-time drone detection in challenging environments.

The Challenge.

Reliable perception is difficult under real-world conditions. Conventional RGB cameras suffer from motion blur, low-light environments, and rapid illumination changes, often resulting in missed detections and reduced system reliability.

Smart Fusion.

Our hybrid fusion architecture combines conventional RGB imagery with high-frequency event data to deliver reliable drone detection where traditional vision systems reach their limits.

sequence_201 train 117 s100.1 M2,174 26.9 s Drone appears 21.9 s after the noise skip. Longest pre-drone gap of all train sequences. Burst ratio 19%.
sequence_127 val 47.5 s99.5 M2,160 12.6 s Validation only. Shortest usable duration (47.5 s). Very high burst ratio (59%). Drone appears 7.6 s after the noise skip.
Camera noise burst — when a Prophesee event camera powers on, unsettled sensor pixels fire continuously for several seconds. All sequences skip the first 5 s to discard this burst; sequence_124 uses start_s = 7.0 due to a residual burst at 5–7 s.
E2VID LSTM warmup — after the burst skip, the reconstruction network takes ~20 s to converge. During this period frames appear degraded, but YOLO detections remain accurate (verified on sequence_85: centre error <5 px throughout).
